Eminence in Action: IBM Success Stories

Driving enterprise AI, digital platforms, and operating model transformations at Global and Local scale.

Global Example
PMI (Philip Morris)
Innovation Highlight
Deployed 4 custom AI assistants (Architect, PM, Developer, Tester) built with specific PMI context to ensure standardized, compliant, and highly relevant IBM Consulting Advantage (ICA) usage.
IBM Impact
Rigorous 2-month education campaign utilizing a Train-the-Trainer (3T) model. Onboarded ~900 trained professionals and achieved 10%–20% efficiency gains across 350 unique users in the first 3 months.
Key Learnings
Customer-specific context is critical for AI adoption. Prompt refinement and correct LLM selection greatly improve effectiveness while mitigating risks associated with enterprise data security.

Local Example
Cathay Pacific
Innovation Highlight
Engineered a comprehensive Digital Platform & CX strategy integrating offline-to-online touchpoints, leveraging contextual 3rd party data to deliver highly personalized passenger interactions.
IBM Impact
Accelerated digital engagement by unifying disparate customer channels. Delivered a seamless Mobile-First Customer Experience that natively engages users within regional media ecosystems.
Key Learnings
Omnichannel consistency requires deeply integrated data products. Merging internal enterprise data with external social/media platforms is the bedrock of modern travel retail transformation.

Local Competitor
AXA Hong Kong
Innovation Highlight
Introduced a RAG-Powered Knowledge Management System (KMS) and an Agentic AI vision, evolving Life Claims operations from scattered files to a centralized enterprise "brain."
IBM Impact
Delivered the first end-to-end Life Claims process documentation across 6 claim types. Provided a concrete 3-year transformation roadmap (2026–2028) spanning Process, Data, Knowledge, and Automation.
Key Learnings
Infrastructure-first is risky without knowledge governance. A use-case-driven approach wins over bottom-up builds. GenAI requires rule-based engines for financial accuracy; it is not a calculator.
