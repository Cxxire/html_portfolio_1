My best movie

Inception

A mind-bending sci-fi masterpiece. I love how it explores the architecture of dreams. The visual effects and the ambiguous ending make it a movie you can watch over and over again without getting bored.

The Thruman Show

A brilliant and surprisingly emotional film. It makes you question reality and the nature of free will. Jim Carrey's performance is incredibly touching, and the concept was way ahead of its time.

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

Absolutely breathtaking animation! Every frame looks like a comic book brought to life. It has a fantastic soundtrack, a heartwarming story about family and self-belief, and the coolest art style I've ever seen.
